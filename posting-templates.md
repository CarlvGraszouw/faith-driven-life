## Posting templates (reusable, warm, not salesy)

### Reels/Shorts templates (5)
1) **Hook → Verse → One step → Prayer**
   - Hook: “If anxiety is loud today…”
   - Verse: (1 line on screen)
   - Step: “One small next step: ___”
   - Prayer: “Jesus, give me peace…”
   - CTA: “Full verses & community → (link) Install for offline access!”

2) **Before/After (testimony tone)**
   - “I felt ___ / Then I read ___ / I did ___ / Peace came slowly…”
   - CTA link to resource + invite testimony page.

3) **3‑point list**
   - “3 verses to carry today…”
   - Each point: reference only (keep video short)
   - CTA to full page.

4) **Saved verses feature**
   - Show “Save verse” button in PWA
   - “Offline encouragement” angle
   - CTA: install + save.

5) **Prayer request invite**
   - “If you need prayer, you don’t have to carry it alone…”
   - CTA: `afaithdrivenlife.com/community.html`

### Carousel templates (3)
6) **5‑slide ‘Start Here’**
   - Slide 1: “Start Here”
   - Slide 2: “Search the Bible”
   - Slide 3: “Daily Verse”
   - Slide 4: “Save verses offline”
   - Slide 5: “Community: prayer + testimonies”

7) **Verse breakdown**
   - Slide 1: verse reference
   - Slide 2: key phrase highlight
   - Slide 3: what it means (warm)
   - Slide 4: one step today
   - Slide 5: prayer + CTA

8) **Forgiveness with boundaries**
   - Slides: Forgive ≠ trust, Forgive ≠ tolerate harm, Forgive = release repayment, Boundaries = wisdom.

### Static graphic templates (2)
9) **Simple verse card**
   - Navy background, gold border, verse reference, 1–2 lines.
   - Footer: “A Faith Driven Life — Connect, Share & Grow”

10) **Community invite**
   - “Need prayer?” + “Share your testimony”
   - URL: `afaithdrivenlife.com/community.html`

- **CapCut**: captions + quick edits for Reels/Shorts
- **Meta Business Suite**: schedule IG + Facebook
- **Later** (free tier): scheduling + link-in-bio
