# Carl's rule (4 Oct 2026): capital letters for God, in every form

"When we speak of God in any tense or form, Him, He, Jesus, always capital letters."

This covers every written thing: scripts, video captions, words drawn in the video, workbooks, show notes, YouTube text, and Bible quotations (adapted from the World English Bible with these capitals).

## Capitalise when the word means God the Father, Jesus or the Holy Spirit
- **Pronouns:** He, Him, His, Himself.
- **God speaking (first person):** Me, My, Mine, Myself, Us, Our (as in Genesis 1:26, "Let Us make man in Our image").
- **Someone speaking to God or Jesus:** You, Your, Yours, Yourself (prayers, and people talking to Jesus).
- **Titles:** King, Great King, Lord, Father, Son, Spirit, Holy Spirit, Messiah, Christ, Word (John 1), Shepherd and Judge, when they name God or Jesus.
- **The Kingdom:** Kingdom of God / the Kingdom (God's kingdom), as the scripts already do.

## Keep lowercase
- people, human kings, Caesar, emperors, Moses, David, Pharaoh, the farmer, the listener ("you" spoken to the audience);
- characters inside Jesus' parables (the king, the master, the father in the story), even when they picture God;
- the general picture of "a king" in the ancient world;
- false gods ("gods"), and relative "who/whom/whose";
- "the king's law" and other human-king phrases.

## How to check
`python3 SP/review/case_guard.py <original> <corrected>` must say "nothing else" changed and lists every change for a second look.
