30 Days of Faith‑Driven Verses

Connect, Share & Grow in Faith · Free verse pack (print or save as PDF)

How to use this pack: Read one verse per day, reflect for one minute, then share it with someone who needs hope.

Day 1

Romans 12:2

“Be transformed by the renewing of your mind…”

Reflection: What thought do you want God to renew today?

Day 2

Isaiah 41:10

“I will strengthen you… I will help you…”

Reflection: Where do you need strength for one small step?

Day 3

Philippians 4:6–7

“In nothing be anxious… and the peace of God… will guard your hearts…”

Reflection: What request can you bring to God right now?

Day 4

1 Peter 5:7

“Cast all your worries on him, because he cares for you.”

Reflection: What worry are you ready to release?

Day 5

John 14:27

“My peace I give to you…”

Reflection: What would change if you received peace as a gift?

Day 6

Psalm 23:1

“Yahweh is my shepherd: I shall lack nothing.”

Reflection: What does it look like to trust God’s provision today?

Day 7

Psalm 46:10

“Be still, and know that I am God.”

Reflection: Take 60 seconds of stillness—what do you notice?

Day 8

Jeremiah 29:11

“To give you hope and a future.”

Reflection: Ask God for hope about one area of your future.

Day 9

Romans 8:28

“All things work together for good…”

Reflection: Where do you need to trust God’s “together” work?

Day 10

Joshua 1:9

“Be strong and courageous… God is with you…”

Reflection: What brave step is God asking of you?

Day 11

2 Timothy 1:7

“Power, love, and self-control.”

Reflection: Which of these do you need most today?

Day 12

Matthew 11:28

“I will give you rest.”

Reflection: What burden can you place in Jesus’ hands?

Day 13

Proverbs 3:5–6

“Trust… and he will make your paths straight.”

Reflection: What does trust look like for you today?

Day 14

Psalm 56:3

“When I am afraid, I will trust in you.”

Reflection: Say it out loud: “I choose trust.”

Day 15

Psalm 34:18

“Yahweh is near to the brokenhearted…”

Reflection: Invite God near to the place that hurts.

Day 16

James 1:5

“Ask… and it will be given.”

Reflection: Ask God for wisdom about one decision.

Day 17

Ephesians 2:10

“Created… for good works.”

Reflection: What good work can you do quietly today?

Day 18

John 3:16

“God so loved… that he gave…”

Reflection: Receive God’s love personally—not generally.

Day 19

Romans 5:8

“Christ died for us.”

Reflection: Thank God for grace you didn’t earn.

Day 20

1 Corinthians 13:4

“Love is patient and kind…”

Reflection: What does patience look like in your home today?

Day 21

Colossians 3:13

“Forgive… as Christ forgave you.”

Reflection: Is there a small forgiveness step you can take?

Day 22

Ephesians 4:32

“Be kind… forgiving…”

Reflection: Who can you bless with kindness today?

Day 23

Psalm 121:1–2

“My help comes from Yahweh…”

Reflection: Ask for help without shame—God welcomes it.

Day 24

Romans 15:13

“Fill you with… joy and peace…”

Reflection: What would “abounding hope” look like today?

Day 25

Isaiah 26:3

“Perfect peace… because he trusts in you.”

Reflection: What thought will you fix on God today?

Day 26

Psalm 147:3

“He heals the brokenhearted…”

Reflection: Where do you need gentle healing?

Day 27

Matthew 6:33

“Seek first God’s Kingdom…”

Reflection: Put God first in one decision today.

Day 28

Acts 22:16

“Calling on his name.”

Reflection: Call on Jesus by name—He hears you.

Day 29

Luke 23:34

“Father, forgive them…”

Reflection: Forgiveness starts as a prayer, even before feelings change.

Day 30

Psalm 23:4

“I will fear no evil, for you are with me.”

Reflection: Where do you need God’s presence to steady you?
