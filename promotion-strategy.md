## Step 4 — Promotion Strategy (low budget, warm tone)

### Goals to measure weekly
- **Resource page views** (anxiety/peace/love/forgiveness/purpose/healing)
- **Email signups** (verse pack)
- **PWA installs** (install prompt shown vs install clicked)
- **Saved verses** (people saving verses = repeat use signal)
- **Community submissions** (prayer/testimony)

---

## Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads — $5–$20/day starter

### Campaign setup (simple)
- **Objective**: Traffic (to the best resource page first)
- **Landing pages** (rotate weekly):
  - `https://www.afaithdrivenlife.com/resources/anxiety-verses?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=anxiety_resource`
  - `https://www.afaithdrivenlife.com/resources/peace-verses?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=peace_resource`
- **Audience**:
  - Age: **18–55**
  - Locations: **US, Canada, UK, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa** (English)
  - Interests: **Christianity, Bible, Bible study, Prayer, Christian music, Jesus, Church**
  - Exclusions: none at first (keep it broad)
- **Budget**:
  - Start: **$5/day** for 3–5 days
  - If CTR is strong (1.2%+) and time on page is good, raise to **$10/day**

### Creative (non-salesy)
- 15s video: “If anxiety is loud today… start here.”
- Static verse card (navy + gold): Philippians 4:6–7
- CTA text: “Read the full verses + short prayer. Install for offline access.”

## Google Ads + Ad Grant
- If you’re eligible as a nonprofit/ministry, apply for **Google Ad Grant** (up to $10k/month).
- Starter keywords:
  - “bible verses about anxiety”
  - “healing scriptures”
  - “bible verses about peace”
- Use tight ad groups by topic; send to the matching resource URL with UTMs.

## Micro‑influencer outreach (5k–30k followers)
Approach: ask for one simple collaboration:
- “Could you share one verse + link to the full resource page? We’ll share your post and pray for your community.”

**How to find accounts (search terms)**:
- “Christian encouragement”
- “Bible verses daily”
- “Prayer community”
- “Christian moms / men of faith / youth devotionals”

**Message template (DM)**
Hi! We run **A Faith Driven Life** — a warm community for Bible verses, prayer requests, and testimonies.  
We made a helpful page on (topic). Would you be open to sharing one verse and linking to it?  
We’d love to share your post and pray for your community too.

Link: (resource URL with UTM)

## Retargeting (install prompt ads)
Once you have enough traffic:
- Build a retargeting audience: visited any `/resources/*` page in last 30 days
- Show a gentle ad: “Install for offline verses + saved gallery”
- Link with UTMs:
  - `https://www.afaithdrivenlife.com/?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=retarget&utm_campaign=install_pwa`
