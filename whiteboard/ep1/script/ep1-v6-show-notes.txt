Episode 1 — What a Kingdom Actually Was

In the last week of His life, Jesus was handed a Roman coin in the crowded temple courts and asked a question that was meant to trap Him, and His answer, "give back to Caesar what is Caesar's and to God what is God's," still asks something of every one of us. This first episode steps into the ancient world of kings, where a boundary stone marked the edge of a king's land and a farmer in Assyria could forget his king for years until the day he needed him. It follows the four ways a king's will reached his people, and it ends on the first page of the Bible, where God places His own image in His land.
