# Episode 1 script: v2 notes

## What was wrong with the original

The facts were sound, but the delivery read like a summary. Sentences were near-identical in length and shape ("The first was... The second was..."), stakes were stated rather than felt, the coin was a prop instead of an object, and sections ended flat instead of pulling forward. A few lines were clipped fragments that land badly when spoken.

## Before / after

1. **Before:** "Someone handed Him a denarius, a small Roman silver coin, worn smooth by many hands. He held it up."
   **After:** "Someone presses a denarius into His hand, a little silver disc worth a day's wages, worn smooth by a thousand fingers. He turns it to the light."
2. **Before:** "The crowd in the temple courts knew what a kingdom was. They lived in one, under Rome."
   **After:** "And when He said *kingdom*, nobody needed it explained, because they were living in one."
3. **Before:** "Then one harvest time the king's officials arrive to claim a share of his crop... His field was held under the king the whole time."
   **After:** "...That field was held under the king the whole time. He could forget the king only because the king was doing his job."
4. **Before:** "One day the prophet Nathan walked into the palace of King David... yet David had to listen."
   **After:** Nathan tells the story of the stolen lamb, David erupts, and Nathan says, "You are the man."

## What changed

- Present-tense storytelling in A; "Whose image?" is set up there and paid off in F with the viewer's own hand.
- Every section ends on a hook into the next.
- Added historical texture (below) and Deuteronomy 17.
- One concrete weekly action replaces three soft ones.
- 1,798 words (original 2,030); section order A–F and on-screen phrases kept.

## Historical claims added (please check)

- Pharisees and Herodians together: Mark 12:13–17.
- Denarius legend "TI CAESAR DIVI AVG F AVGVSTVS" (son of the divine Augustus); worth a day's wage: Matthew 20:2.
- Roman garrison in the Antonia fortress beside the temple: Josephus, *Jewish War* 5.238–247; Acts 21:31–35.
- Babylonian *kudurru* boundary stones (Istanbul Archaeology Museums): chapter source.
- Suzerain–vassal treaties (Hittite and Assyrian): standard scholarship, e.g. Kitchen, *On the Reliability of the OT*.
- "Thus says the great king": 2 Kings 18:19 (Assyrian herald).
- Assyrian royal statues in conquered cities; corvée labour and crop levies: standard Assyriology.
- Deuteronomy 17:18–20; 31:10–13; Leviticus 25:23; Psalm 24:1; Luke 17:21; Luke 10:16; 2 Samuel 12:1–7; Exodus 20:1; Genesis 1:26–27; Mark 1:15.
