Practical application (peace you can practice)

  • Peace in your breathing: inhale “God is with me,” exhale “I can trust Him.”
  • Peace in your mind: pick one verse and read it out loud twice.
  • Peace in your next step: do one small obedient action today.
  • Peace in community: don’t carry it alone—share a prayer request.

A short prayer for peace

Lord Jesus, You are the Prince of Peace. Quiet my heart and steady my thoughts. Help me trust You with what I cannot control, and lead me in what I can obey today. Let Your peace guard my heart and mind. Amen.
