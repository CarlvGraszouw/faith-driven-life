Practical application (strength for today)

  • Pick one verse and read it morning + night for 7 days.
  • Ask for prayer—we carry burdens together.
  • Take wise steps (rest, hydration, medical care) without shame.
  • Speak hope—even a whisper: “God is with me.”

A short prayer for healing

Father, You are near to the brokenhearted. I ask for healing and strength—body, mind, and spirit. Give wisdom to the helpers around me and peace in my heart. Let hope rise again. In Jesus’ name, amen.
