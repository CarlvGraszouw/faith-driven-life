How to apply these verses today (in 5 minutes)

  1. Name the worry. Write one sentence: “I feel anxious about ___.”
  2. Choose one verse. Read it slowly twice. Let it be louder than your fear.
  3. Turn it into prayer. Replace “you” with “me” and speak it to God.
  4. Take one small step. Text a friend, drink water, take a walk, or do the next right thing.
  5. Connect. If you want, share a prayer request or testimony—this is a community.

A short prayer for anxiety

Father, You see what I’m carrying. I bring my worries to You right now. Please guard my heart and mind with Your peace. Give me wisdom for my next step, and strength for today. Help me trust You—one moment at a time. In Jesus’ name, amen.
