Practical application: love in community

  • Receive love: read one verse and say, “God’s love includes me.”
  • Repair one relationship: send one honest, gentle message.
  • Serve one person: one small act of kindness today.
  • Share: if God is working in you, your testimony can help someone else.

A short prayer for love

Father, thank You that You love me first. Heal what’s wounded in me, and teach me to love with truth and kindness. Help me forgive quickly, listen well, and serve with joy. Let Your love shape our community. Amen.
